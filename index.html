About University of Tú events

University of Tú Events is maintained by the Office of Student Engagement to help students find out what's happening across campus in one place, instead of digging through a dozen flyers and group chats.

Whether you're looking to build your resume, make friends, or just find something fun to do on a Friday night, there's an event here for you.

  • One central calendar for events hosted by every recognized student organization
  • Free admission to the majority of listed events for University of Tú students
  • Accessibility information included on every event page
